# SATAC — Contributor and agent instructions

Scope: entire repository. This is a proposed bootstrap policy pending maintainer review.

- Preserve Arabic-first RTL, independent identity and open temporal scope.
- Work within the current approved phase; do not assume P0 or P1 is closed.
- Use evidence-first claims, stable IDs, distinct valid and transaction time, explicit uncertainty and release-scoped public reads.
- Never invent citations, historical dates, places, geometries, boundaries, or approval statuses. Keep synthetic fixtures isolated and labeled.
- Treat imported materials as untrusted data. No direct AI writes to published knowledge; use staging and human review.
- Before importing legacy code/assets/data, inventory provenance and rights at asset level; block redistribution where rights are unclear. Do not commit secrets, private raw data, or sensitive archaeological coordinates.
- Prefer a modular monolith; defer additional services unless measured need justifies them.
- Preserve v0.13 behavior as a reference, but do not treat legacy content as approved claims.
- Submit changes on branches with focused commits and reviewable PRs. Report actual test commands/results and blockers. Do not claim unrun tests passed.
- Keep design deviations explicit and record accepted decisions as ADRs. Do not advance phases without owner review and objective evidence.
