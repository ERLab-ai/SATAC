## ADR-010 — Owner-directed publication and correction policy
Publication is independent of verification and source completeness; correct known errors directly with audit, keep administrative states private, preserve scientific uncertainty. Authorized publication service only; no synthetic or known incorrect public values. See v1.2 policy. Implementation details and tests remain pending.
